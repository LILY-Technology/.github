# Support

**Customers** — support is inside the Lily Delivery app, or on WhatsApp at the
customer-service number shown there. GitHub issues are not read by the support
team and an order problem posted here will not be seen in time.

**Vendors and drivers** — contact your Lily representative, or the vendor portal's
support link.

**Developers working on these repositories** — open an issue in the repository
concerned, or ask in the team's channel. Use the issue templates: a report with
the version and the exact request/response is answerable; «it is broken» is not.

**Security** — do not open an issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
